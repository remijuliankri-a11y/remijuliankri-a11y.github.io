Dashboard walkthrough

The master's thesis produced a 5-page interactive Power BI dashboard for a Norwegian professional association. Each page serves a distinct decision-support purpose, from population-level risk overview to individual member explanation. All member values are anonymized.

Page 1 of 5
Overview

The first page gives managers a fast population-level picture before diving into detail. It combines KPI cards, a risk distribution donut, geographic map, and economic exposure toggle — all designed to answer "where are we?" in seconds.

  • Risk band distribution across Low, Moderate, and High
  • Economic exposure toggle: expected loss vs revenue at risk
  • Geographic map showing risk concentration by local branch
  • Predicted date indicator — scores are point-in-time, not permanent
Persistent shell
Navigation & information layer

A persistent sidebar spans all five pages. It includes a reset-filter button, predicted date, and an information button that opens plain-language explanations on demand — keeping the main interface clean while making guidance accessible.

  • Five-page progressive navigation always visible
  • Reset filters to prevent hidden context from distorting interpretation
  • Plain-language info panels triggered on demand, not by default
Page 2 of 5
Risk segments

Managers can compare risk across segments like age group, billing frequency, education phase, or local branch. A ranked bar chart shows which segments carry the highest average churn risk, while a bubble plot adds segment size to separate high-risk from large-but-low-risk groups.

  • Six segmentation dimensions selectable via filter panel
  • Ranked bar chart for quick risk prioritization across segments
  • Bubble plot to distinguish risk level from organizational scale
  • Segment-level SHAP explanation panel populated on selection
Interaction flow
Segment-level explanation

Selecting a segment in the bar chart or bubble plot populates an explanation panel below. It shows the top risk-increasing and risk-reducing drivers for that group, giving managers a plausible account of why a segment appears risky — not just that it is.

  • Step 1: choose segmentation dimension
  • Step 2: select a segment in chart or bubble plot
  • Step 3: explanation panel appears with SHAP-based drivers
Page 3 of 5
Member list

Once the task shifts from understanding patterns to selecting individual cases, the member list provides a ranked table with search and filter options. Risk-band tabs let managers move between High, Moderate, and Low priority groups.

  • Ranked table with churn score, risk group, product, and branch
  • Search field and operational filter panel for targeted lookup
  • Risk-band tabs for prioritization view switching
  • Member-level summary card populated on row selection
Interaction flow
Member-level detail & explanation

Selecting a member populates a summary card with their risk score, rank, profile, engagement over the past 180 days, and the top 5 SHAP-based risk drivers. This gives advisors the context needed for a justified follow-up decision — not just a score.

  • Risk score, predicted probability, and rank in risk band
  • Member profile: age group, product, local branch, billing frequency
  • Days since arrival and recent participation indicators
  • Top 5 individual SHAP drivers with plain-language labels
Page 4 of 5
Risk drivers

The global explanation page shows which features the model relies on most across the entire scored population. A SHAP beeswarm plot visualizes how each feature affects churn risk, with a plain-language reading guide built directly into the dashboard.

  • Global feature importance cards ranked by SHAP weight
  • Separate views for drivers that increase vs reduce risk
  • SHAP beeswarm plot with built-in "how to read" explanation panel
  • Membership length, employer info, and billing frequency as top drivers
